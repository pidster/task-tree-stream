## What the founder must decide before approval

1. **The cross-project cycle question is now a real design fork, not a detail.** A17 rejected an organisation-wide serialisation point on throughput grounds; blocker 2 shows per-project is unsound once E-01's option 2 is switched on. The choices are: take the org point only when a write crosses projects, take the graph version of every project in the reachability closure, or accept asynchronous cycle detection for cross-project edges (A12, currently rejected). This changes the data model and belongs to the founder, not to D02.
2. **`Decision.rationale` is required, immutable and unredactable** (D01 major, D09 major). The E-02 decision rests on redaction being the removal path; it does not reach the one text field the design makes permanent. Options: make `rationale` redactable like `Transition.reason`, or accept that a decision rationale is permanent and say so in §5.4.
3. **Confirm the four cuts above**, and confirm the two verb gaps are D09's to close in v3 rather than new escalations: un-archive / pause / resume verbs, and read verbs for dependencies and edges.
4. **`agent_tier` / `agent_runtime` now belong to no document.** Either add a row to the Concept ownership map or confirm they are cut.
